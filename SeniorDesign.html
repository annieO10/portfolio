Recreation Program Trip Planning and Management System

Our team was tasked with creating a trip planning service to coordinate trip details, and make joining and finding trips more accesible. Our client is the Recreation Services at Iowa State University. They need a more organized way to keep track of trip-goers medical information or dietary restrictions. Our website would allow them to feature trips students can join, convey information about a trip in an engaging way, and organize trip-goers' information. Finding and joining trips will be more accesible to students, and information will be clear and easy to find. Eventually we would expand the scope of our project to allow multiple organizations to signup and create trips.

My Role and Expectations

I am the UI/UX design lead for this project. Meaning I mostly work on the frontend to create pages, get information from backend, and plan different functions for this website. I am excited to get experience working with a team on a project this large. I am also looking forward to expanding my web development skills and getting more creative with this project. By the end of the project I hope to see all the functions I have put forward to the group being developed. I also hope I help to create an interesting and engaging design for the UI.
